PRIVATE PERSONAL AUTOMATION

Hermes Assistance

A private, single-user automation service for calendar summaries and work-email alerts.

Personal use only

What it does

Hermes Assistance connects to the owner's Google account to read calendar information and monitor selected work-email activity. It uses that information only to generate automations requested by the account owner, such as next-day schedule summaries and new-email alerts.

Access

Access is restricted to the owner. This is not a public application or a service offered to other users.

Privacy

Google account data is used only to provide the owner's configured automations. It is not sold or used for advertising.

PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Overview

Hermes Assistance is a private, single-user personal automation service. It is operated solely for the Google account owner's own use and is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

The service may access Google Calendar data and Gmail data authorized by the owner. This can include calendar names and events, Gmail message history, message metadata, recipient information, and message content needed to produce configured summaries and alerts.

How data is used

Google data is used only to perform owner-configured automations, including next-day calendar summaries and alerts for selected work email. Selected content may be processed by the owner's configured automation and AI providers and delivered through owner-selected private notification channels.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and automation state are stored on systems controlled by the owner. Access is restricted through local operating-system permissions, private network controls, and application credentials. The service does not sell Google user data and does not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers needed to run the owner's requested automation, such as Google APIs, the configured AI provider, and the owner's selected private notification channels. It is not shared for advertising or independent third-party use.

Retention and deletion

Automation outputs and operational state are retained only as needed for the owner's personal workflows. The owner can stop the service, delete locally stored data, or revoke Google access at any time from the Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
